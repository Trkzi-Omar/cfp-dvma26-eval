# Nimbus subscriptions and charges

Nimbus offers Free, Pro, and Enterprise plans. Paid plans are billed monthly or
annually to the payment method on file.

- You are charged on your renewal date. The date is shown in **Settings >
  Billing**.
- If you were **charged twice** for the same period, this is a duplicate charge.
  Duplicate charges are refunded to the original payment method. Open a billing
  request and include both charge dates.
- Failed payments are retried for 7 days before the plan is downgraded to Free.
