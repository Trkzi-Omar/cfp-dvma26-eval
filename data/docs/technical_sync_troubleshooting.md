# Troubleshooting Nimbus sync and sign-in

If files are not syncing or you cannot sign in to the desktop app:

1. Confirm you are online and can reach the Nimbus web app.
2. Sign out and sign back in to refresh your session.
3. Clear the local cache from **Preferences > Advanced > Clear cache**.
4. Update to the latest app version.

If sign-in fails with an "invalid credentials" error, reset your password from
the web sign-in page. If sync is stuck on a single file, pause and resume sync.
