# Deleting your Nimbus account

You can permanently delete your Nimbus account from **Settings > Account >
Delete account**. Deletion removes your profile, files, and shared links.

- Deletion is irreversible. Export anything you want to keep first.
- After you confirm, the account enters a 7 day grace period during which you can
  cancel the deletion by signing in again.
- After the grace period, data is removed within 30 days.
