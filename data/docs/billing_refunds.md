# Nimbus refunds

If you are not satisfied with a paid plan, you can request a refund.

- Refunds are available within **30 days** of the charge.
- Duplicate charges are always refunded in full, regardless of the window.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method and take 5 to 10 business
  days to appear.

> Note: this document states a 30 day window. The support refund policy states a
> 14 day window. This contradiction is intentional in this reference repo and is
> what the conflicting-documents scenario exposes.
