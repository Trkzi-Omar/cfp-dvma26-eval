# Resetting your Nimbus password

If you cannot sign in because you forgot your password, you can reset it yourself.

1. Go to the Nimbus sign-in page and click **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address on your account.
3. We send a password reset link to that email. The link expires after 60 minutes.
4. Open the link and choose a new password.

If the reset email does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder
and confirm you entered the email associated with your account. Reset links are
single use. If a link has expired, request a new one from the same page.
