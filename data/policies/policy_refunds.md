# Support policy: refunds

- Standard refunds may be granted within **14 days** of the charge.
- Duplicate charges are always refunded in full.
- Refunds beyond 14 days require manager approval and must be escalated.

> Note: the customer-facing refund documentation states a 30 day window. This
> policy states 14 days. The contradiction is intentional in this reference repo.
> When docs and policy disagree, the system should surface the conflict and
> escalate rather than pick one silently.
